## Important interpretation notes

- International visitor arrivals count arrivals, not necessarily unique persons.
- Place of residence is not nationality.
- This dataset is an official tourism indicator, but it does not measure resident wellbeing,
  site-level crowding, visitor behaviour, or visitor sentiment.
- Any totals, definitions, notes, revisions, and rounding statements from the original
  SingStat download must be retained and reviewed before analysis.

## Important interpretation notes

- Figures refer to gazetted hotels.
- Average occupancy rate is the percentage of available rooms sold during a period.
- Average room rate is the average rate paid for rooms sold, excluding service charge and government taxes.
- Hotel performance does not directly measure resident wellbeing, site-level crowding,
  visitor behaviour, cultural respect, environmental impact, or sentiment.
